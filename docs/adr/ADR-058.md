# ADR-058: The person page leads with the person's life (amends ADR-053)

## Status

Implemented

- **Status:** built on `claude/person-page-life-events-d11a0a` and deployed. No migration.
- **Context:** the page opened on a long Family list, and the facts sat below it as one white panel per kind, each a single fact in a real tree. A marriage, the event most researchers look for after birth and death, was not among them: it belongs to the partnership and showed only behind "Dates & status". The right column spent five cards on empty states.
- **Decision:**
  - **A Life section first.** The page shows the person's events and their partnerships' events (engagement, marriage, separation, divorce, annulment) as one list, oldest first, undated last. A partnership event names the partner and links to the partnership, which owns its evidence and editing. The merge is a pure function (`life-timeline.ts`) over the DTO the page already loads.
  - **Every fact is shown.** Nothing sits behind "Show N earlier" on the page, so a conflict is always in place beside the fact it disagrees with. A fact's citations, notes and controls share one line.
  - **Attributes apart.** Occupation, residence, education and the other attributes are not events. They follow under "Also recorded", without a "Date unknown" on each. A fact whose kind is not known to be an attribute is placed as an event, so none can drop out of view.
  - **The rest of the record in one card.** Notes, Names, Photos and documents, Tasks, Privacy and History share one section card in the right column, divided by rules, instead of a card each. An empty media list is one line beside Upload.
  - **The drawer keeps ADR-053's order and grouped facts,** newest shown: in a narrow panel, the latest of each kind is the quicker answer. The shared pieces it renders do change with the page: an empty media or notes list is one line beside its action, and the separators and "(approximate)" keep their spaces.
- **Rejected alternatives:** a timeline above the grouped facts (every fact said twice); keeping the page's sections in the drawer's order (the life is what a reader came for, and it was below the fold); including children's births (a child's summary carries its birth as text without a sort key, so they could not be placed without parsing every one in the browser or changing the DTO).
- **Consequences:** the page and drawer no longer share a section order, so ADR-053's "one section order" holds for the drawer only. A move control in the timeline swaps a fact with a neighbour of the same date that is also an event, or also an attribute, and up means earlier. Children's births in the timeline would need a sortable birth on `PersonSummaryDto`.
