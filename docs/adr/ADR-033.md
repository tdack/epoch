# ADR-033: Ball, Ribbons and Orrery join Rings as selectable WebGL views (extends ADR-032)

## Status

Implemented

- **Status:** accepted by the owner and deployed; built on 2026-10-08. Checked in Chromium only (headless, SwiftShader WebGL) and by eye on the owner's 314-person family tree.
- **Context:** the owner proposed three more 3D ideas to compare with Rings: a hyperbolic "Poincaré ball", braided lineage "ribbons" and a gravitational "orrery". The plan, each view's notes, timings and rough edges are kept locally and are not in the repository.
- **Decision:**
  - **Three more `LAYOUT_MODES` values:** `poincare` (labelled Ball), `ribbons` and `orrery`, stored in the existing `view_mode` columns (no migration: they are text).
  - **Switcher:** the layout control is **Flat | 3D**, with a picker beside 3D for Rings, Ball, Ribbons or Orrery. The last 3D view is remembered per browser (`localStorage`), so the 3D button returns to it. Withdrawn modes (Layers, Floors) still show when in force, as ADR-032 set out.
  - **Per-mode traits:** `VIEW_MODE_TRAITS` in `src/lib/shared/view-mode.ts` holds, per mode, whether it is WebGL, asks for the wider groups, has an arrival depth, can edit, and falls back to Flat on a phone. It replaces the checks that named Layers and Rings one by one.
  - **Shared stage and shell:** `three-stage.ts` (renderer, orbit controls with the `change` redraw from ADR-032, on-demand frames, picking, projection, fat lines, framing, disposal) and `WebGlTreeShell.svelte` (name buttons, keyboard, camera presets, spin, All names, reduced motion, WebGL failure) were extracted from Rings. Every WebGL view, Rings included, is a pure layout module plus a scene drawn on them.
  - **Ball:** height is calendar time, oldest at the top. Each person's era comes from their recorded birth or death date bounds and is relaxed across partnership and parent–child links (`eras.ts`), so an undated partner lands in their partner's era. Distance from the axis is `R_slice · tanh(spread)`, so wide generations compress toward the glass instead of clipping. Estimated eras only place people; they are never shown as dates, and a private person's dates are never read.
  - **Ribbons:** flow runs oldest to youngest. Partners' tubes, in `union_partner` order, braid into a glowing knot for their union, and children's tubes leave it. A person with several unions splits to each knot. Tubes are merged into a few draw calls; only the nodes are pickable.
  - **Orrery:** a seeded, fixed-iteration force simulation inside the pure layout, so the same graph always gives the same picture. Partners hold as a binary near 4 units apart, children are drawn to their parents' midpoint, and a person with several unions holds several binaries. Orbital drift is decoration only, and is off under reduced motion.
  - **Neutral model throughout:** a union is never labelled a marriage, partners are not assumed to be one of each sex, and sex colours use the existing tokens, including unknown.
- **Revised after the owner's first look (same day):**
  - The Ball draws its spheres at half the radius the layout spaces them for, so the opening view is not crowded.
  - The Orrery has a **Motion** switch, remembered per browser, that holds it at its laid-out picture.
  - Every WebGL view shows a **hover card** beside the pointer: name, recorded years, sex, place on the focus line, and partnerships and children in view. It never shows the Ball's estimated eras, and private people get none.
  - The viewer can **clear the highlight**: clicking empty space, Escape once no drawer is open, or the **Focus line** switch. Everyone stays where they are and is drawn alike; a name or the switch lights the line again. Loading the whole tree with no focus was considered and set aside: it would reload and re-lay out the view, a jarring jump, and it would be the first read not bounded by the view's depth.
- **Accessibility:** as ADR-032 for every WebGL view. Names are buttons, Enter marks the person, Shift+Enter centres on them and opens the drawer and private people cannot be picked. None of the views edits.
- **Consequences:** no new dependency, because three.js already ships in Rings' lazy chunk. Layout of about 250 drawn people takes under 30 ms in each view; Orrery's first, cold call is around 70 ms (see the notes). `playwright.config.ts` reads `E2E_PORT`, so parallel worktrees can each test their own server. Left for later: a neutral `lineage.ts` instead of importing `ringLineage` from `rings.ts`, and a shared merged-line helper for Ribbons and Orrery. The owner kept all four views (Rings, Ball, Ribbons and Orrery) in the 3D picker.
