# ADR-081: The flat tree prints as one poster cut to its drawing, and downloads as a PDF built in the browser

## Status

Implemented

- **Status:** accepted and built on 2026-10-28, and deployed. No migration. It extends ADR-080,
  whose reports are otherwise left to the browser's print.

## Context

The flat tree canvas printed only a note pointing to a chart. Its cards are readable only at a size
no standard sheet holds once a tree is more than a few families wide: the owner's whole tree is
about 6.8 m long at readable size. The 3D views would need a tight zoom and every name turned on
to be worth printing, so they are left out.

Printing alone was not enough. The macOS and iOS print dialogs, which an installed app uses, offer
only the printer's paper and ignore the page size the page asks for. Chrome's own print preview
honours it.

## Decision

- **One sheet, cut to the drawing** (`tree/poster.ts`). Cards print at 0.25 mm per layout unit,
  about 53 mm wide, so names are about 10pt and dates 8.5pt. The sheet is the drawing plus 10 mm
  margins, turned to suit its shape, and never smaller than A4. Only the short side is held to
  A0's 841 mm: a wide tree runs on as a banner rather than shrinking its cards, and shrinks only
  when even its short side is wider than that.
- **The poster is drawn only while it is needed.** `TreeCanvas` renders the same scene snippet at
  full detail into a second SVG, moved to the end of `<body>`, and `app.css` prints it alone on a
  named page whose size is set in a `<style>` added for the print (CSS cannot take a variable inside
  `size`). It is drawn before the dialog opens, never from `beforeprint`: during a real print Chrome
  refuses the Trusted Types call Svelte makes to build new markup, so a poster drawn there printed
  blank and took the page to the error screen.
- **Print, or Download PDF.** A printer button in the zoom bar prints, and Cmd-P or Ctrl-P is caught
  to do the same. Its menu adds Download PDF, built in the browser with jsPDF and svg2pdf.js:
  - The poster's computed styles are written onto a copy as attributes, because svg2pdf reads
    neither `var()` nor Svelte's scoped classes, and Chrome reports a variable-set length as
    `calc(3px)`.
  - DejaVu Sans Condensed (regular and bold) is embedded, because PDF's built-in fonts have no ♂,
    ♀, ⚧ or ○ and only Western European letters. Condensed, because the cards truncate names to fit
    the narrower screen font.
  - A sheet past PDF's 5,080 mm page limit (which jsPDF and Acrobat both enforce) is cut into equal
    panels along its long side, one per page, that butt together.
  - The libraries and fonts (about 2 MB) load only on a download, and the service worker leaves the
    fonts out of its precache (ADR-077).
- **No server PDF.** Cloudflare Browser Rendering would match the screen exactly, but needs a binding,
  per-hour billing and an authenticated render path (reports research, section 7).

## Consequences

- The poster holds the people the view has loaded: a reader widens the generations or turns on the
  whole tree first.
- The browser's File, Print menu bypasses the button, so it prints the note telling the reader to use
  it.
- The PDF's typeface differs a little from the screen's, and names keep the cards' truncation.
- Tested in Chrome only. Safari's print may ignore the custom page size, and the download has not
  been tried in Safari or in the installed app on macOS or iPhone.
